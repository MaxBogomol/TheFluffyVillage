Universal Woodworking - Adds more compatibility for Woodworks Minecraft data pack
Author:
OnixTheCat:

About:

This Minecraft datapack adds more recipe variants for Sawmill from TeamAbnormals' Woodworks Mod to make it compatible with other mods.

Datapack also adds some QoL recipes both for Vanilla crafting and Sawmill.


Compatibility:

- Another Furniture: Recipes for mod's furniture;
- Farmer's Delight: Recipes for cabinets;
- Malum: Recipes for mod's wood types and custom wooden blocks;
- Wizard's Reborn: Recipes for mod's wood types and custom wooden blocks;
- Silly Oddities: Recipes for mod's compatibility content.
